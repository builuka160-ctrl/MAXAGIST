
## 5. Smoke tests

Open:

```text
https://maxagist.com/?lang=ru&utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=test&gclid=TEST123
https://maxagist.com/?lang=lv&utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=test
https://maxagist.com/massage-pinki-saliena.html?lang=en
https://maxagist.com/vyezd.html?lang=ru
```

Verify:

- no language gateway when `?lang=` exists;
- right language is shown immediately;
- CMP appears for a fresh browser;
- reject keeps Google consent denied and does not load Meta Pixel;
- accept updates consent and loads Meta Pixel;
- WhatsApp click is `whatsapp_click`, not `generate_lead`;
- form backend success fires exactly one `lead_success` / Google Ads conversion;
- failed form / WhatsApp fallback do not fire lead conversion;
- `gclid`/UTM appear on the lead in Telegram admin;
- lead status can move to booked/visited/paid and optional EUR revenue can be saved;
- viewer account cannot open leads/editor;
- `?setup=...` returns 503 if SETUP_SECRET is intentionally unset.
